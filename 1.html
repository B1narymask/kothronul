Lesson 6: To be & Pronouns

In this lesson you're gonna learn all the pronouns and their respective conjugation of verb to be

person Koþronul conjugation possessive
1sg (I) tov frod tîs
2sg (you) muþ ûþu uþui
3sg-fem (she) xief cet bis
3sg-masc (he) yat cet vis
1pl-in (inclusive we) ropsó gria yun
1pl-ex (exclusive we) rôpso gria wam
2pl (y'all) uþru hslê tris
3pl (they) asvú pi pþa

Examples

Frod ati koriuks

"I'm a child"

rôpso gria blosvenata fatorors

"We (exclusive) are eating bread"
